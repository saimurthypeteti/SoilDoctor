What Should I Do? (Step-by-Step Action Plan)

  1. Step 1: Remove and safely burn or compost-away heavily spotted lower foliage to halt spore splash.
  2. Step 2: Apply protective copper-based or Mancozeb fungicide spray early morning.
  3. Step 3: Apply nitrogen-rich fertilizer (Urea or organic enriched vermicompost) to restore vegetative vigor.
  4. Step 4: Transition to drip irrigation; avoid sprinkler foliage wetting.
  5. Step 5: Re-scan crop foliage after 14 days to confirm blight containment.

Organic & Biological Treatment

Apply certified copper hydroxide (Bordeaux mixture 1%). Spray cold-pressed neem oil (5ml/L water) every 7 days. Drench soil with liquid jeevamrutha and incorporate 5 tons/acre of farmyard manure to regenerate soil organic carbon.

Conventional Chemical Treatment

Foliar spray Mancozeb 75% WP (2.5g/L water) or Chlorothalonil. Side-dress with Urea (46% N) at 25 kg/acre followed by light irrigation. Alternate with systemic Azoxystrobin (1ml/L) if disease persists during rainy spells.

Field Treatment Guide

Best Spray Time Early morning (6:00 - 8:30 AM) when wind is calm.
Watering Protocol Water at ground level. Keep leaves completely dry.
Protection Equipment Wear gloves and mask during spray application.
